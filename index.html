About Us

WHY WOMEN EMPOWERMENT?
Over the past decade, gender equality has emerged as a critical factor not only for the health of nations but also for their social and economic advancement.
Promoting gender equality and women empowerment is central to the UNDP’s Sustainable Development Goals.
Women empowerment focuses on ensuring equal rights, opportunities, and access to resources for women, allowing them to make independent decisions and take charge of their own lives.
When women empowerment is prioritized, women significantly contribute to economic growth, social development, and a sustainable future.
Empowered women take active roles in leadership, education, healthcare, and decision-making at various levels.
Women empowerment helps create a more inclusive and balanced society where women’s voices are valued, their skills are recognized, and their potential is fully realized.
Ultimately, women empowerment is essential for building a fairer and more harmonious world for everyone.
WHAT WE DO?

Rise Up's women empowerment programme Swabhiman, initiated in 2005, reaches out to marginalized and socially excluded women.
It focuses on interventions in nutrition, healthcare, and livelihood.
The programme capacitates women through innovative community practices, empowering them to seek healthcare and bring sustainable changes in the community.
It also supports them to become aware, skilled, and financially independent.
Swabhiman actively works to create a gender-equal society, engaging men and boys in the process of women empowerment.
Rise Up is a non-profit organization working tirelessly to promote gender equality through awareness programs, education, and community outreach. Right from the establishment, we collect and utilize all types of donations and charities for the welfare of unprivileged people.
We clearly believe that all human beings are equal and have the right of enjoying good health along with a proper standard of living.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create a just and equitable society where all individuals—regardless of gender—have equal access to rights, resources, opportunities, and safety.
We work to empower women, girls, and gender-diverse communities by providing education, economic and digital skills, legal protection, health services, and leadership training. We aim to eliminate gender-based violence, challenge discriminatory norms, and promote inclusive policies through advocacy, research, and community engagement.
Through partnerships and evidence-based programs, we strive to build sustainable systems that uphold dignity, ensure participation, and foster long-lasting change."
We aim to create a society where everyone has equal rights, opportunities, and respect regardless of gender.

A. Promoting Equal Rights and Social Justice
An NGO should work to:
Advocate for laws and policies that protect women’s rights.
Challenge discriminatory norms and stereotypes across communities.
Promote gender-sensitive governance and representation.
B.Preventing Gender-Based Violence (GBV)
A core mission area for most gender-focused NGOs:
Prevent domestic violence, sexual harassment, trafficking, child marriage, and workplace harassment.
Provide survivor-centered support such as counseling, legal aid, safe shelters, and rehabilitation.
Engage men and boys as allies against violence.
C. Economic Empowerment and Financial Independence
Economic inequality is central to gender inequality.
NGOs should work to:
Provide vocational and entrepreneurial training.
Promote women’s employment, equal pay, and workplace rights.
Support access to microfinance and digital banking.
D. Health, Reproductive Rights, and Well-Being
Gender equality cannot exist without bodily autonomy and access to health services.
Mission may include:
Ensuring reproductive health services and menstrual hygiene awareness.
Educating communities on maternal health, nutrition, and mental health.
Addressing harmful practices like FGM, early marriage, or stigma around reproductive rights.

SDG 5

Sustainable Development Goal 5 (SDG 5) focuses on achieving gender equality and empowering all women and girls. It recognizes that gender equality is not only a fundamental human right but also a crucial foundation for a peaceful, prosperous, and sustainable world. Despite progress in recent decades, women and girls around the world continue to face discrimination and violence, as well as social, economic, and political barriers that limit their full participation in society. SDG 5 aims to address these inequalities by eliminating all forms of discrimination and gender-based violence, including human trafficking, sexual exploitation, child marriage, and female genital mutilation. It also highlights the need to recognize and reduce unpaid domestic and care work, which disproportionately affects women, by promoting shared household responsibilities and improving access to public services and childcare. Additionally, SDG 5 calls for ensuring women’s full participation and equal opportunities in leadership roles across political, economic, and public spheres. It emphasizes the importance of universal access to sexual and reproductive health and rights, which remain essential for women’s health, autonomy, and well-being. Economic empowerment is another key aspect, with the goal of providing women equal rights to financial resources, property ownership, technology, and entrepreneurship opportunities. Increasing women’s access to information and communication technologies is particularly important, as it supports education, employment, and innovation, helping to bridge the digital divide. Achieving SDG 5 requires strong gender-responsive policies, laws, and budgeting systems that promote equality and protect women’s rights. Ultimately, SDG 5 plays a transformative role in shaping a fairer and more inclusive world. When women and girls are empowered, entire communities benefit through improved economic productivity, healthier families, stronger environmental stewardship, and greater social stability. Gender equality is therefore a key driver of progress across all other Sustainable Development Goals, making SDG 5 essential to the success of the entire 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development.
